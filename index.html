Prologue Story first

When everyone can build, the story becomes the differentiator.

Anyone can build. Tools are cheap, talent is everywhere, and that idea you had a few days ago can become a reality in minutes. It's precisely because building has become easy that, now, the product is not enough of a differentiator. The story is key.

Narrative isn't a coat of paint you add once you've scaled. It's the thing that makes growth legible to the people watching: customers, hires, investors. Get it wrong and the cost is visible — prospects who ghost you, discovery calls that never move to the next stage, pitches that don't land at events.

Building was never the hard part. Selling is; and selling without a story is impossible. That's where we start.
